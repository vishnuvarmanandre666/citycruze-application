# CityCruze Hyderabad Launch Plan

## 1. Launch approach

Start with a controlled pilot in selected Hyderabad zones. Expand only
after measuring booking demand, eligible vehicle supply, vendor
reliability and contribution margin.

Potential zones to research: - HITEC City. - Gachibowli. - Madhapur. -
Kondapur. - Financial District / Nanakramguda. - Kukatpally.

These are proposed research zones, not verified demand rankings.

## 2. Phase 0: Validate the operating model

Before launch: - Obtain transport-law advice. - Confirm vendor and
vehicle eligibility. - Confirm self-drive rental insurance. - Confirm
payment and vendor settlement structure. - Finalize customer and vendor
agreements. - Confirm GST and other tax treatment. - Complete privacy
and security review.

## 3. Phase 1: Supply discovery

-   Interview local fleet operators.
-   Understand vehicle categories and pricing.
-   Assess available eligible inventory.
-   Verify partner operating capability.
-   Identify pickup/return procedures.
-   Negotiate pilot agreements.

## 4. Phase 2: Closed pilot

-   Invite a limited set of approved partners.
-   Activate only approved eligible vehicles.
-   Test real operational workflows under professional sign-off.
-   Manually monitor each booking.
-   Capture customer and vendor feedback.
-   Fix availability, handover and payment issues.

## 5. Phase 3: Controlled public launch

-   Launch in selected service zones.
-   Publish transparent prices and rental terms.
-   Use measured local acquisition campaigns.
-   Monitor vendor response and fulfilment daily.
-   Pause unreliable listings quickly.
-   Track contribution margin by channel and vehicle category.

## 7. Pilot targets to validate

Potential planning targets: - 10--15 approved partners. - 30--50
eligible vehicles. - Three to five service zones. - Defined support
coverage. - Complete handover/return records. - Daily payment
reconciliation.

These are proposed targets, not evidence of market demand or guaranteed
achievable outcomes.

## 8. Go/no-go criteria

Do not expand unless: - Vehicle and operator compliance is confirmed. -
Insurance is valid for actual use. - Booking availability is reliable. -
Customer support can handle incidents. - Payment and settlement
reconciliation works. - Unit economics are understood. - Vendor
fulfilment meets agreed standards.
