# CityCruze Vendor Onboarding

## 1. Objective

Onboard legitimate fleet partners and only activate vehicles verified as
eligible for the intended self-drive rental activity.

## 2. Vendor onboarding stages

1.  Account creation and mobile/email verification.
2.  Business and beneficial-owner information collection, where
    applicable.
3.  Identity and address checks.
4.  Business registration and operator authorization review, where
    applicable.
5.  Bank account verification.
6.  Agreement acceptance.
7.  Vehicle-by-vehicle submission.
8.  Document and insurance review.
9.  Physical or approved third-party inspection where required.
10. Admin approval and listing activation.

## 3. Vendor information

-   Legal name and trading name.
-   Contact person and authorized signatory.
-   Business address.
-   Contact details.
-   Business constitution and registration details, where applicable.
-   Tax details as advised by finance/legal professionals.
-   Bank account information.
-   Fleet size and operating locations.
-   Relevant licences and authorizations.

## 4. Vehicle information

-   Registration number.
-   Make, model, variant and year.
-   Fuel type and transmission.
-   Seating capacity.
-   Odometer reading.
-   Vehicle photographs.
-   Registration certificate.
-   Insurance policy and permitted use.
-   Permit/licence documents as applicable.
-   Fitness and pollution documents where applicable.
-   Maintenance and inspection records.
-   Rental restrictions and included kilometres.

## 5. Approval statuses

Vendor: - DRAFT - SUBMITTED - UNDER_REVIEW - ACTION_REQUIRED -
APPROVED - REJECTED - SUSPENDED

Vehicle: - DRAFT - SUBMITTED - DOCUMENT_REVIEW - INSPECTION_PENDING -
APPROVED - REJECTED - SUSPENDED - EXPIRED

Only APPROVED vendors and APPROVED vehicles may be published, subject to
all other eligibility and availability checks.

## 6. Review controls

-   Validate authenticity where verification sources are available.
-   Check consistency across registration, insurance and permit records.
-   Confirm insurance covers the actual self-drive rental use.
-   Track expiry dates and suspend listings when mandatory documents
    expire.
-   Record reviewer, decision, date and reason.
-   Restrict access to sensitive documents.
-   Do not rely solely on automated OCR.

## 7. Vendor agreement topics

Have counsel draft the agreement to cover: - Legal eligibility and
representations. - Vehicle ownership or authority to list. - Insurance
and claims obligations. - Pricing and commission. - Availability
accuracy. - Booking acceptance and fulfilment. - Vehicle condition and
maintenance. - Customer handover and return. - Cancellation and refund
responsibilities. - Data protection and confidentiality. - Indemnity,
liability and dispute resolution. - Suspension and termination.

## 8. Vendor performance

Track: - Response rate. - Acceptance rate. - Fulfilment rate. -
Vendor-caused cancellation rate. - Vehicle condition complaints. -
Document compliance. - Customer ratings. - Settlement exceptions.
