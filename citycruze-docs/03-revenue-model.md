# CityCruze Revenue Model

## 1. Primary revenue stream

The proposed primary revenue stream is commission on completed eligible
bookings.

Illustrative example: - Rental charge: ₹3,000 - Marketplace commission
assumption: 15% - CityCruze gross commission: ₹450 - Partner share
before other agreed deductions: ₹2,550

This example excludes taxes, gateway fees, discounts, refunds,
chargebacks, incentives and operating costs. The commercial agreement
must define the commission base precisely.

## 2. Proposed partner pricing

These are hypotheses to test, not market-validated rates.

  Partner segment                 Illustrative commission
  ---------------------- --------------------------------
  Small fleet partner                             12--15%
  Medium fleet partner                         Around 15%
  Large fleet partner      8--12%, subject to negotiation

Avoid arbitrary segmentation. Set rates based on support costs, partner
volume, service quality and acquisition economics.

## 3. Possible secondary revenue

Only introduce after marketplace liquidity is established: - Optional
vendor subscription plans. - Sponsored vehicle placement with clear
advertising labels. - Corporate account services. - Partner tools and
analytics. - Optional operational services where actually delivered.

Do not charge for services that are not provided or imply that paid
placement guarantees bookings.

## 4. Unit economics

Track each booking separately: - Gross booking value (GBV/GMV). -
Commission revenue. - Discounts funded by CityCruze. - Payment
processing costs. - Refunds and chargebacks. - Customer support and
verification costs. - Partner incentives. - Contribution margin.

A useful simplified formula:

`Contribution per completed booking = recognized marketplace revenue - variable booking costs`

Have a CA determine accounting and GST treatment. GMV is not CityCruze
revenue.

## 5. Illustrative scenario model

Assumptions: - 100 active vehicles. - 8 bookings per vehicle per
month. - Average rental charge ₹3,000. - Commission assumption 15%.

Calculation: - Bookings: 100 × 8 = 800. - GMV: 800 × ₹3,000 =
₹24,00,000. - Gross commission: ₹24,00,000 × 15% = ₹3,60,000.

This is a hypothetical steady-state scenario, not a forecast. It
excludes expenses, tax, cancellations, refunds, unavailable days and
seasonality.

## 7. Pricing governance

-   Store the price quote and its components at booking time.
-   Define whether taxes, delivery, excess kilometres, deposits and
    add-ons are included.
-   Prevent vendors from changing a confirmed booking price
    unilaterally.
-   Maintain a versioned commission schedule.
-   Record who approved manual adjustments.
-   Show customers the complete payable amount before payment.
