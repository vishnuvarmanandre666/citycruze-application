# CityCruze Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand foundation

-   **Name:** CityCruze
-   **Tagline:** Every Journey. Your Way.
-   **Descriptor:** Self-drive car rentals
-   **Brand promise:** A clear, convenient way to discover and book
    eligible self-drive rentals.

## 2. Brand personality

-   Confident, not boastful.
-   Modern, not overly futuristic.
-   Helpful, not pushy.
-   Trustworthy and transparent.
-   Energetic while remaining practical.

## 3. Visual identity direction

### Colour palette

  Token           Hex         Intended use
  --------------- ----------- ------------------------------------
  Midnight Navy   `#111820`   Primary dark background and header
  Cruze Orange    `#F5A623`   Primary accent and calls to action
  White           `#FFFFFF`   Main light surfaces
  Silver Grey     `#9CA3AF`   Secondary text and neutral UI

Use orange for emphasis, not for large areas of body text. Maintain
accessible contrast for text and controls.

### Typography

Suggested starting point: - Headings: Poppins, bold or semibold. - Body
and UI: Inter, regular or medium. - Use a consistent type scale and
avoid excessive all-caps text.

### Logo direction

Create a distinctive wordmark: - "CITY" and "CRUZE" as one readable
brand name. - Orange accent on "CRUZE". - Optional custom "C" symbol
incorporating a road or motion curve. - Prepare horizontal, stacked,
monochrome and app-icon variants.

Avoid copying vehicle manufacturer marks or using a generic car
silhouette as the only distinctive element.

## 4. Voice and messaging

Preferred language: - "Browse eligible self-drive cars." - "Review the
rental terms before booking." - "Your booking request is awaiting
confirmation." - "See the total price before you pay."

Avoid: - Guaranteed availability before inventory confirmation. - Claims
such as "fully insured" unless verified for the specific vehicle and
rental use. - "Cheapest in India" or other unsubstantiated superiority
claims. - Implying CityCruze owns partner vehicles if it does not.

## 5. Suggested UI language

-   Primary CTA: "Explore cars"
-   Vendor CTA: "List your cars"
-   Search CTA: "Find available cars"
-   Booking CTA: "Continue to booking"
-   Partner portal: "Vendor dashboard"

## 6. Brand protection

Before public launch: 1. Conduct an Indian trademark search for
identical and similar marks. 2. Review relevant classes with a trademark
professional, especially vehicle rental/transport and marketplace
services. 3. Review similar spellings and phonetic variants. 4. Secure
approved domains and social handles. 5. Obtain legal advice before
filing a word mark or logo mark.

Domain availability does not establish trademark rights.
