# CityCruze Business Overview

## 1. Executive summary

CityCruze is a self-drive car-rental marketplace. It will initially
serve Hyderabad and may expand to additional Indian cities after
validating supply, demand, compliance and unit economics.

CityCruze is intended to connect customers with independent fleet owners
and rental operators. It is not assumed that CityCruze owns the
vehicles.

## 2. Positioning

-   **Brand:** CityCruze
-   **Category:** Self-drive car rental marketplace
-   **Tagline:** Every Journey. Your Way.
-   **Initial market:** Hyderabad, Telangana
-   **Expansion ambition:** Multiple Indian cities
-   **Primary supply:** Approved fleet partners
-   **Primary customer groups:** Residents, visitors, families and
    business travellers

## 3. Value proposition

### For customers

-   Discover eligible self-drive vehicles in one place.
-   Compare vehicle details, rental terms and total price.
-   Check availability before booking.
-   Complete a clear booking and payment process.
-   Access booking support and documented handover/return procedures.

### For fleet partners

-   Reach additional customers.
-   Manage vehicle listings, pricing and availability.
-   Receive structured booking requests.
-   Review booking and settlement records.
-   Access operational support and performance information.

### For CityCruze

-   Earn marketplace commission and, later, optional partner-service
    revenue.
-   Build a trusted supply network.
-   Develop repeat customer relationships and operational data.

## 4. Marketplace operating principle

CityCruze should list only vehicles and operators whose eligibility for
the intended self-drive rental activity has been verified. A document
upload alone is not approval. Admin approval must follow documentary
checks and any required physical or third-party inspection.

## 5. Initial scope

### MVP

-   Customer web application.
-   Vendor portal.
-   Admin portal.
-   Vendor and vehicle verification workflow.
-   Vehicle search and availability.
-   Booking lifecycle.
-   Payment provider integration in sandbox.
-   Refund and settlement records.
-   Notifications and audit logs.

### Later releases

-   Native mobile apps.
-   Loyalty and referral programme.
-   Corporate accounts.
-   Dynamic pricing.
-   Multi-city operations.
-   Advanced fleet analytics.

## 6. Non-goals for the first release

-   CityCruze-owned fleet operations.
-   Chauffeur-driven bookings.
-   International expansion.
-   Complex bidding or auction mechanisms.
-   Unreviewed automated approval of vehicle documents.

## 7. Key assumptions to validate

1.  Fleet partners can legally supply eligible self-drive rental
    vehicles.
2.  Suitable insurance cover is available for the intended rental use.
3.  A compliant marketplace/payment settlement structure can be
    established.
4.  Customers in launch zones have sufficient demand.
5.  Partners can maintain accurate availability and respond to booking
    requests.
6.  Commission levels support customer acquisition, operations and
    payment costs.

## 8. Success criteria for the pilot

-   Approved and active eligible vehicles.
-   Reliable availability accuracy.
-   High vendor response and fulfilment rates.
-   Low preventable cancellations.
-   Positive contribution margin per completed booking.
-   Strong customer satisfaction and repeat intent.
