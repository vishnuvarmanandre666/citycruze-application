# CityCruze Customer Journey

## 1. Discovery

Customer visits CityCruze and selects: - Pickup city and location. -
Pickup date and time. - Return date and time. - Optional vehicle
category and preferences.

Search results must show only vehicles eligible and available for the
requested period.

## 2. Vehicle comparison

Each listing should clearly show: - Make, model and variant. - Actual
vehicle photographs. - Transmission, fuel and seating. - Pickup/return
arrangements. - Included kilometres and excess-kilometre charges. -
Deposit, if applicable. - Insurance/excess terms in plain language. -
Cancellation terms. - Total estimated price.

Do not display unsupported claims such as guaranteed insurance coverage.

## 3. Customer verification

Before booking or handover, apply the verification policy approved by
legal and risk teams. This may include identity and driving-licence
checks, eligibility rules, age requirements and fraud screening.

Collect only necessary information and protect uploaded documents.

## 4. Booking

1.  Customer selects vehicle.
2.  Backend rechecks availability.
3.  System creates a temporary inventory hold.
4.  Customer reviews final terms and price.
5.  Customer completes required verification.
6.  Payment is initiated.
7.  Payment provider confirms status through a verified server-side
    event.
8.  Vendor confirmation is obtained if required.
9.  Booking is confirmed and instructions are sent.

## 5. Handover

Record: - Customer and authorized driver verification. - Vehicle
identity. - Date/time and location. - Odometer and fuel/battery level. -
Existing damage with photographs. - Accessories and keys. - Customer
acknowledgement.

## 6. During rental

Provide: - Booking details and support contact. - Breakdown/incident
instructions. - Extension request process. - Late-return policy. - Rules
for permitted use and prohibited use.

## 7. Return

Record: - Return date/time and location. - Odometer and fuel/battery
level. - New damage, if any. - Missing accessories. - Inspection
photographs. - Customer/vendor acknowledgement.

## 8. Completion

-   Calculate disclosed additional charges, if any.
-   Resolve disputes through a documented process.
-   Update booking to completed.
-   Initiate settlement according to the approved policy.
-   Invite customer feedback.
-   Send invoice/receipt as applicable.

## 9. Customer support

Support should have access to booking status and relevant evidence, but
not unrestricted access to identity documents or payment secrets.
