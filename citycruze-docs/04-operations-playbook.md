# CityCruze Operations Playbook

## 1. Operating model

CityCruze coordinates a marketplace of approved self-drive rental
partners. Operations must protect customer safety, vehicle eligibility,
booking accuracy, payment integrity and partner accountability.

## 2. Core teams and responsibilities

  Function                 Responsibility
  ------------------------ -----------------------------------------------
  Marketplace operations   Daily booking monitoring and escalations
  Vendor success           Partner onboarding and performance
  Compliance reviewer      Document and eligibility checks
  Customer support         Booking questions, changes and complaints
  Finance operations       Reconciliation, refunds and settlements
  Admin                    Access control, policy and exception approval

One person may perform multiple functions during a pilot, but sensitive
actions should still have appropriate approval and audit trails.

## 3. Daily operating checklist

### Start of day

-   Review bookings due for pickup and return.
-   Check vendor response queue.
-   Review vehicle/document expiry alerts.
-   Check payment and webhook failures.
-   Confirm support coverage.

### During the day

-   Monitor pending vendor confirmations.
-   Resolve availability conflicts.
-   Verify customer support escalations.
-   Record handover and return evidence.
-   Track incidents and late returns.

### End of day

-   Reconcile payments and booking statuses.
-   Review refunds and failed settlements.
-   Escalate unresolved disputes.
-   Review next-day pickups.
-   Produce an operations summary.

## 4. Booking exception handling

### Vendor does not respond

-   Send configured reminder.
-   Expire or escalate request after response deadline.
-   Do not mark as confirmed without required confirmation.
-   Notify customer and offer alternatives or refund where applicable.

### Vehicle unavailable after confirmation

-   Pause the listing.
-   Contact customer promptly.
-   Offer a suitable alternative only with customer consent.
-   Process refund according to the published policy if no acceptable
    alternative exists.
-   Record root cause and partner performance impact.

### Customer no-show

-   Follow the disclosed no-show policy.
-   Capture contact attempts and timestamps.
-   Do not invent charges outside the accepted terms.

### Accident or breakdown

-   Prioritize personal safety and emergency services.
-   Follow insurer and vehicle operator procedures.
-   Preserve evidence and incident records.
-   Escalate to designated operations lead.
-   Do not make unsupported liability promises.

### Late return

-   Contact customer and vendor.
-   Apply only disclosed and lawful charges.
-   Record actual return time and evidence.
-   Escalate vehicle availability impact.

## 5. Service standards to pilot

Initial operational targets to validate: - Vendor response within a
defined service window. - Customer support acknowledgement within a
published period. - Every handover has a condition record. - Every
refund has a reason and audit trail. - Every vehicle listing has current
approval status.

## 6. Incident management

Classify incidents: - P1: Safety, suspected fraud, data breach, payment
compromise. - P2: Vehicle unavailable at handover, serious breakdown,
major payment issue. - P3: Routine booking changes, minor complaints or
listing corrections.

Maintain incident owner, timestamps, actions, customer communications
and closure reason.
