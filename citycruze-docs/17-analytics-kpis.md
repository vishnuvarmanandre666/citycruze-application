# CityCruze Analytics and KPIs

## 1. Marketplace health

### Supply

-   Registered vendors.
-   Approved vendors.
-   Submitted vehicles.
-   Approved active vehicles.
-   Eligible available vehicle-days.
-   Vehicle document compliance rate.

### Demand

-   Search sessions.
-   Vehicle detail views.
-   Quote requests.
-   Checkout starts.
-   Paid bookings.
-   Completed bookings.

### Conversion

-   Search-to-detail rate.
-   Detail-to-quote rate.
-   Quote-to-booking rate.
-   Payment success rate.

## 2. Marketplace operations

-   Vendor response time.
-   Vendor acceptance rate.
-   Booking fulfilment rate.
-   Vendor-caused cancellation rate.
-   Customer cancellation rate.
-   Vehicle unavailability incidents.
-   On-time handover rate.
-   Return inspection completion rate.
-   Support tickets per booking.

## 3. Financial metrics

-   GMV: total customer rental transaction value under the chosen
    definition.
-   Recognized commission revenue.
-   Average booking value.
-   Commission take rate.
-   Payment processing cost.
-   Refund rate.
-   Chargeback rate.
-   Vendor payable.
-   Settlement ageing.
-   Contribution margin per completed booking.

## 4. Retention

-   Repeat booking rate.
-   Customer cohort retention.
-   Vendor retention.
-   Bookings per active customer.
-   Customer satisfaction and complaint rate.

## 5. Vehicle utilization

Define utilization carefully:

`Booked eligible hours ÷ Available eligible hours`

Exclude periods when a vehicle is not legally or operationally
available. Do not count suspended or document-expired vehicles as active
supply.

## 6. Dashboard principles

-   Define every metric in a data dictionary.
-   Show date range and timezone.
-   Separate gross booking value from platform revenue.
-   Reconcile finance metrics to the ledger.
-   Avoid double-counting cancelled or refunded bookings.
-   Restrict personal data in analytics views.
