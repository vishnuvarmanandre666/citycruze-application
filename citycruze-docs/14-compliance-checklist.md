# CityCruze Legal and Compliance Checklist

## Important notice

This is a planning checklist, not legal advice or a determination that
CityCruze or any proposed vehicle is licensed or eligible. Indian
transport, insurance, consumer, privacy, tax and payment requirements
must be reviewed by qualified professionals before operations.

## 1. Transport and vehicle eligibility

Obtain a written legal assessment covering: - Applicable Motor Vehicles
Act provisions. - Rent-a-Cab Scheme and applicable amendments/rules. -
Telangana state requirements and transport department practice. -
Whether CityCruze itself requires an operator licence or other
authorization. - Which licences/authorizations each fleet partner must
hold. - Vehicle registration category and permitted use. - Permit and
fitness requirements. - Driver/customer eligibility requirements. -
Geographic operating restrictions. - Records that must be maintained.

Do not assume an ordinary privately registered vehicle can be listed for
paid self-drive rental.

## 2. Insurance

For every vehicle, verify: - Policy is valid. - Rental/self-drive use is
disclosed and covered. - Permitted drivers and use are clear. -
Third-party and own-damage coverage are understood. - Deductibles/excess
and exclusions are disclosed. - Claims procedure and responsibility are
contractually clear.

A standard private-car policy must not be assumed to cover commercial
rental use.

## 3. Customer and driver verification

Have counsel and insurers define: - Minimum age and experience
requirements. - Driving-licence validity and verification. - Identity
verification. - Additional driver policy. - Fraud and risk checks. -
Document storage and retention.

## 4. Marketplace and contracts

Prepare and review: - Customer terms of service. - Privacy notice. -
Cancellation and refund policy. - Vendor agreement. - Vehicle listing
declaration. - Handover/return checklist. - Damage and incident
procedure. - Grievance and support process. - Liability and dispute
provisions.

Contracts must accurately reflect CityCruze's actual role and must not
misrepresent who owns, operates, insures or supplies a vehicle.

## 5. Payments and tax

Obtain professional advice on: - Payment aggregator/marketplace
eligibility. - Collection and settlement of vendor funds. - GST
registration and treatment. - E-commerce operator obligations, where
applicable. - Tax invoices and credit notes. - TDS or other withholding,
where applicable. - Accounting treatment of GMV and commission. - Refund
and chargeback allocation.

## 6. Privacy and technology

Review applicable Indian data-protection law and rules, including
requirements relevant to: - Notice and consent/legal basis. - Purpose
limitation. - Data minimization. - Security safeguards. - Retention and
deletion. - Grievance handling. - Processor/vendor contracts. - Data
breach response.

## 7. Trademark and brand

-   Search identical and similar marks.
-   Review relevant trademark classes with counsel.
-   Review phonetic variants.
-   Check company and domain names separately.
-   Secure domains only after brand-risk assessment.
-   Consider word-mark and logo-mark filing.

Domain availability does not prove trademark availability.

## 8. Launch gate

Do not accept live bookings until the responsible legal, insurance, tax
and payment professionals have signed off the operating model and launch
controls.
